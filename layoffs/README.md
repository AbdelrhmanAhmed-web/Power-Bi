## 📊  Global Layoffs Analysis Project (2020-2023)

This is the final stage where the cleaned data and SQL insights were transformed into an interactive visual story. The dashboard is designed to provide both high-level executive summaries and granular deep-dives.

### 🎨 Dashboard Structure
The report consists of a professional cover page and two main analytical pages:

1.  **Cover Page:** An introductory landing page featuring the project scope and navigation buttons to guide the user.
2.  **Global Overview:** * **Key KPIs:** Total layoffs, average percentage, and global impact.
    * **Geographic Insights:** Map visuals showing the most affected countries.
    * **Trend Analysis:** Line charts tracking layoffs over the years and months.
3.  **Analysis & Breakdown:**
    * **Decomposition Tree:** An advanced AI visual used to break down layoffs by Industry, Company Stage, and Location.
    * **Scatter & Comparison Charts:** Analyzing the relationship between Funding and Layoff intensity.

### 💡 Key Features Implemented:
* **Dynamic Navigation:** Custom buttons with hover effects for a seamless user experience (Ctrl + Click to navigate).
* **Slicer Panel:** Flexible filters for Industry, Country, and Date ranges without cluttering the UI.
* **Data Consistency:** Connected directly to the **SQL Final View** to ensure data integrity and automated formatting.
* **Smart Narratives:** Integrated AI summaries to provide instant text-based insights.
